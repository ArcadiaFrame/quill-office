# Third-party assets

Assets added to this fork on top of the upstream Euro-Office sources. Everything listed
here is licensed for redistribution with this AGPL-3.0 project. No Microsoft-owned icons,
fonts, sounds or images are used.

## Templates

The Task Launcher references the templates that already ship with the app
(`desktop-apps/common/templates`); nothing is copied. The 18 additional templates
(`aero/templates/build_templates.py`: letters, flyers, newsletter, reports, budget,
planners, presentations…) are original work written for this project, and their icons are
drawn by `aero/plugins/make_icons.py`. They only use the bundled OFL fonts listed below.

## Quill Office branding (original work)

- `aero/icon/quill-office.svg` (app icon) and everything rendered from it, the installer side images
  and theme previews (`aero/installer/build_installer_art.py`): original artwork made for this project,
  licensed with the project. They replace the stock ONLYOFFICE-branded installer images.
  Lettering uses Open Sans (SIL OFL 1.1, `core-fonts/opensans/LICENSE.txt`).
